Introduction
Introduction & Navigation
General structure of the application and interface markers

ItriX Farm is a web-based farm execution management system (MES). It centralises real time monitoring of greenhouse production, crop traceability, commercial management, resource control and financial management on one platform.

Seizing a new harvest

  1. Click on " + KNOW RECOLATE" (high right) or "+ RECOLTE" in the top bar.

  2. Select greenhouse of origin and the variety harvested.

  3. Enter the gross quantity, date and brake/gauge measurements.

  4. Assign Market(s) destination to create dispatches.

  5. Add the selling price — the lot is entered into status CLOSED.

⚠️Priceless lots appear in the tab No price and do not contribute to the confirmed CA. They must be charged quickly for up-to-date accounting.

Creating a plantation

  1. Click on "+ PLANTATION" (high right purple button).

  2. Select the growing season active, greenhouse available and the variety.

  3. Seizing the surface (m2) and the expected performance (kg/m2) — theoretical production is calculated automatically.

  4. Indicate date of planting and validate.

💡The total theoretical production at the top of the page (orange) is the sum of all plantings. It serves as a reference objective for the growing season. Click on the icon

Module 04 · Piloting
Agronomy — Cultural Journal
Full traceability of technical interventions on crops

The Journal Agronomique is the technical logbook of crops. Each intervention (fertilization, plant protection treatment, irrigation, size...) is timed and linked to a specific greenhouse and variety.

Journal Agronomique
Fertilization intervention on greenhouse S1 (MIRRAVIA) on 24/03/2026, product test01, dose 50, quantity 3 L, 5 workers. Columns EC and pH allow to plot the parameters of the nutrient solution.

Save intervention

  1. Click on "+ INTERVENTION" (top right or top bar).

  2. Select greenhouse and variety concerned.

  3. Select the type of intervention in the drop-down list.

  4. Inform the product, the dose per m2 and total quantity.

  5. (Optional) Enter EC, pH, on Number of workers and Notes.

  6. Validate — the intervention is recorded with automatic time stamping.

Appendix A
First start workflow
Recommended Initial Configuration — 13 Steps to Be Operational

To configure the application from scratch, follow this order of entry. Each step depends on the previous one to ensure consistency of data.

  1. Farms → Farms: Create the farm of the domain (root geographical entity).

  2. Operation → Locks: Add each greenhouse with its identifier, surface and attachment farm.

  3. Exploitation → Varieties: Create the catalogue (VITALIA, MIRRAVIA...) with reference yields.

  4. Exploitation → Campaigns: Create the growing season with planting dates, beginning and end of harvest.

  5. Resources → Suppliers: Register suppliers by category with payment deadlines.

  6. Resources → Stocks: Create items with alert thresholds and unit costs. Enter the initial stock via + IN.

  7. Trade → Customers : Register buyers (exporters, local markets) and their conditions.

  8. Trade → Markets : Define destination markets (EXPORT, LOCAL...) for dispatches.

  9. Parameter → Chart of Accounts & Budgets: Configure cost categories and budget envelopes.

  10. Production → Create plantations: Record each plantation (severe × variety × surface × yield × date).

  11. Harvests → Seize the first harvest: Create lots with weight, greenhouse, variety and destination market.

  12. Agronomy → Journalize interventions: Record the fertilizations and treatments with the water.

  13. Trade → Orders & Invoices: Create customer orders and generate invoices for tracking the CA.

✅Once these 13 steps are complete, the Executive Dashboard displays all indicators in real time. KPIs automatically update each new entry in the application.
